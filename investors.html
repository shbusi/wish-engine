One tower. The whole world stacks it.

Humans everywhere stack stones to make a wish. Wish Cairn is one shared 3D cairn: a stone a day is free, the next ones cost a few cents, and half of every stone sold is paid out to CAIRN stakers in the stablecoin it was bought with.

How money flows

Investors are paid from revenue, not from a buyback or a promise. When stone sales rise, distributions rise the same day. Revenue is distributed per stablecoin, so there is no conversion loss. Web payments (PayPal) are converted to USDC by the operator and deposited into the same pool.

The CAIRN token

Fixed supply of 1,000,000,000 CAIRN, minted once at deployment. No mint function, no owner, no pause, no blacklist, no transfer tax.

What the token does

    Public sale

    The sale contract holds the USDC until the window closes. If the soft cap is missed, every buyer gets a full refund and no tokens leave the contract. Unsold tokens return to the treasury.
